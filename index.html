Year 5 English Revision Notes

🏙️ Unit 1: Towns and Cities (Comparatives & Superlatives)

Learn how to compare places, buildings, and cities using short and long adjectives.

1. Comparative Adjectives (Comparing 2 things)

Add -er + than for short words. Use more + adjective + than for long words.

  • • Kuala Lumpur is bigger than Ipoh.
  • • Modern buildings are more expensive than old houses.

2. Superlative Adjectives (Comparing 3+ things)

Use the + -est for short words. Use the most + adjective for long words.

  • • The Petronas Twin Towers are the tallest in the city.
  • • It is the most beautiful park in Malaysia.

🐯 Unit 3: Wild Life & Present Continuous

Express actions happening right now and describe animal abilities.

Present Continuous Tense (is/am/are + Verb-ing)

The eagle is flying in the sky right now.

The monkeys are climbing the tall trees.

Animal Verbs & Key Vocab

• Camouflage: Hiding by blending with surroundings.

• Habitat: The natural home of an animal.

• Predator: An animal that hunts other animals.

🥗 Unit 5: Food and Health (Countable & Uncountable)

Countable Nouns (Can count with numbers)

Use many or a few.

Example: apples, sandwiches, eggs, vegetables.
• How many apples do you have?

Uncountable Nouns (Cannot count easily)

Use much or a little.

Example: water, rice, sugar, soup, juice.
• There isn't much water left in the bottle.

⚽ Unit 4: Sport Rules (Play, Do, Go & Good At)

PLAY

Team sports with a ball/equipment:

• Play football, tennis, basketball

DO

Individual sports & martial arts:

• Do gymnastics, taekwondo, athletics

GO

Activities ending in -ING:

• Go swimming, cycling, skiing

⏳ Unit 8: Past Simple Tense (Regular & Irregular Verbs)

Regular Verbs (+ed)

• play ➔ played

• visit ➔ visited

• walk ➔ walked

Irregular Verbs (Change spelling)

• go ➔ went | buy ➔ bought

• win ➔ won | eat ➔ ate

• see ➔ saw | take ➔ took

UASA Writing Guide (Parts 6 & 7)
Part 6 • Short Note / Email (30-50 words)

Email Format & Openers

In UASA Part 6, you must write a short email or message to a friend or family member answering 3 prompts.

📌 Model Template:

To: sara@email.com

Subject: Sports Club Join

Hi Sara,

How are you? I would love to join the sports club with you this Saturday. We can play badminton together at the sports hall. Don't forget to bring your racket!

See you soon,
Amir

Part 7 • Guided Writing (60-80 words)

Guided Essay Checklist

In UASA Part 7, use all given notes or pictures to write a coherent story or article in past tense.

✨ Key Writing Tips:

  • 1. Past Tense Verbs: Use went, enjoyed, bought, saw, played.
  • 2. Time Connectors: Use First, Next, Then, After that, Finally.
  • 3. Adjectives: Add descriptive words like delicious, crowded, wonderful, thrilling.
